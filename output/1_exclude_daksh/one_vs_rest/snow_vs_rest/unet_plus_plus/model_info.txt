UNet++ — One vs rest: snow
dataset: 1. exclude_daksh: trained and cross-validated on the 4 patch dates

MODEL
  UNet++ (segmentation-models-pytorch 0.6, PyTorch 2.14, RTX 3080)
  ResNet-34 encoder with ImageNet weights, 1,500 steps of 8 random 256 x 256 crops
  centred near labelled pixels, random 90-degree turns and flips, cross-entropy
  weighted by 1/sqrt(class frequency) on labelled pixels only, AdamW lr 1e-3 with
  one-cycle schedule, mixed precision. Sees the neighbourhood (shape, texture).
  Weights + input scaling: unetpp_final.pt.

INPUTS
  11 per pixel: SR_B2, SR_B3, SR_B4, SR_B5, SR_B6, SR_B7, ST_B10, water_occ, NDWI, NDSI, NDVI

DATA
  training dates: 2023-07-24, 2023-10-04, 2024-04-21, 2024-06-22
  test dates:     2023-07-24, 2023-10-04, 2024-04-21, 2024-06-22
  training pixels: not_snow 84,794, snow 564
  evaluation: 5-fold spatial cross-validation (64-row east-west bands, 12-row buffer), then one final model on all labels for the maps
