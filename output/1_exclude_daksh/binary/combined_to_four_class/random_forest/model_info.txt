Random Forest — Binary snow + river merged back to four classes
dataset: 1. exclude_daksh: trained and cross-validated on the 4 patch dates

MODEL
  Random Forest (scikit-learn 1.9)
  300 trees, minimum 2 samples per leaf, class weights balanced per tree
  (balanced_subsample). Each pixel is classified on its own inputs, no neighbourhood.
  Not trained itself: the binary snow and river models' answers merged
  (river & no snow = river, river & snow = ice, snow off-river = snow, else land).

INPUTS
  11 per pixel: SR_B2, SR_B3, SR_B4, SR_B5, SR_B6, SR_B7, ST_B10, water_occ, NDWI, NDSI, NDVI

DATA
  training dates: 2023-07-24, 2023-10-04, 2024-04-21, 2024-06-22
  test dates:     2023-07-24, 2023-10-04, 2024-04-21, 2024-06-22
  training pixels: river 39,991, ice 1,732, snow 564, land 43,071
  evaluation: 5-fold spatial cross-validation (64-row east-west bands, 12-row buffer), then one final model on all labels for the maps
