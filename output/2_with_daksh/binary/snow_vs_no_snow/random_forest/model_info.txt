Random Forest — Binary: snow vs no snow (snow = ice + snow)
dataset: 2. with_daksh: trained and cross-validated on the 4 patch dates + daksh 2024-03-12

MODEL
  Random Forest (scikit-learn 1.9)
  300 trees, minimum 2 samples per leaf, class weights balanced per tree
  (balanced_subsample). Each pixel is classified on its own inputs, no neighbourhood.

INPUTS
  9 per pixel: blue, green, red, nir, swir1, water_occ, NDWI, NDSI, NDVI

DATA
  training dates: 2023-07-24, 2023-10-04, 2024-03-12, 2024-04-21, 2024-06-22
  test dates:     2023-07-24, 2023-10-04, 2024-03-12, 2024-04-21, 2024-06-22
  training pixels: no_snow 83,102, snow 2,788
  evaluation: 5-fold spatial cross-validation (64-row east-west bands, 12-row buffer), then one final model on all labels for the maps
